# Metrics

Computed quantities derived from snapshots, signals, news, and analyses.

- [unrealized P&L](unrealized-pnl.md) — per-share / percent / total P&L
- [realized P&L](realized-pnl.md) — locked-in gain/loss from closed lots (stocks/gold/funds)
- [position status](position-status.md) — emoji-tiered P&L bucket
- [day change %](day-change-pct.md) — intraday move vs previous close
- [distance from 52-week range](distance-from-52w.md) — vs 52w high/low
- [volume ratio](volume-ratio.md) — volume vs prior-day baseline
- [market signal](market-signal.md) — price/volume → MINOR/MAJOR tier
- [news sentiment score](sentiment-score.md) — LLM bullish/bearish score
- [recommendation accuracy](recommendation-accuracy.md) — backtest of past recommendations
